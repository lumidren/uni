# Lab Data Protection Directive (LDPD) v1 — Unseen Standard for Ingestion Demo

This fictional directive is used to demonstrate standard-neutral ingestion. It is
not a real law. Its clauses are written to overlap with — and in two places
conflict with — the preloaded frameworks.

Article 1. Controllers shall encrypt all personal data at rest and in transit using strong cryptography.
Article 2. Access to personal data shall be restricted using least privilege and multi-factor authentication.
Article 3. Audit logs of access to personal data shall be produced and retained for a minimum of seven years.
Article 4. Personal data of national residents shall be stored within the country and shall not be transferred outside its borders.
Article 5. Controllers shall notify the supervisory authority of any personal data breach within 48 hours of discovery.
Article 6. A documented incident response and containment procedure shall be maintained and tested annually.

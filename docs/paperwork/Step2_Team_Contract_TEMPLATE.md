## 1. Goals
- Deliver three functioning, independently-assessable systems (Red, Blue, GRC)
  plus the integrated Purple Team platform.
- Develop skills in: offensive tooling & verification, ML-based detection and
  explainability (XAI), GRC/risk modelling, and multi-system (LLM/agent)
  orchestration.
- Every member understands and can defend **every** module, not only their own.

## 2. Expectations
- **Attendance:** attend `<e.g. two>` team meetings per week; notify the team
  `<e.g. 12h>` in advance if unable to attend.
- **Participation:** each member owns at least one subsystem area and reviews
  another's work.
- **Communication:** respond on the team channel within `<e.g. 24h>`.
- **Quality:** code is commented, tested where practical, and runnable before it
  is merged.

## 3. Policies & Procedures
- **Meeting cadence:** `<day/time>` weekly, plus ad-hoc before milestones.
- **Communication channel:** `<e.g. WhatsApp / Discord / Teams>`.
- **Version control:** Git on `<repo URL>`; short-lived branches; pull requests
  reviewed by at least one other member before merge; small, frequent commits.
- **Code review:** no self-merge of non-trivial changes; reviewer checks it runs.
- **Decision-making:** consensus first; if blocked, majority vote; the team lead
  breaks ties.

## 4. Consequences (non-performance)
1. **Verbal reminder** from the team lead.
2. **Documented written warning** (logged in the team channel) if unresolved.
3. **Escalation to the instructor** with the documentation, if still unresolved.
Contribution is tracked in the repo's "Division of work" so issues are evidenced.
